# 📊 TechMart Sales & Profit Analysis | Power BI

## 📌 Project Overview

This project focuses on analyzing TechMart's sales data using Power BI to evaluate overall business performance, identify profitable products, analyze regional sales, and understand order trends.

The goal was to transform raw sales data into an interactive dashboard that helps management make data-driven business decisions.

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies

* **Microsoft Power BI** – Dashboard Development & Data Visualization
* **Power Query** – Data Cleaning & Transformation
* **DAX** – Measures and KPI Calculations

## ❓ Business Problems

* Which region generates the highest sales?
* Which products contribute the most revenue?
* Which category generates the highest profit?
* Who is the top-performing salesperson?
* Which states contribute the most profit?
* What is the distribution of delivered, returned, and cancelled orders?
* How do sales and profit change over time?

## 📈 Key Findings

* **Total Sales:** ₹5.15 Crore
* **Total Profit:** ₹60.17 Lakh
* **Total Orders:** 2,000
* **Total Customers:** 40
* **Top Sales Region:** North
* **Top Revenue Product:** Laptop
* **Most Profitable Category:** Electronics
* **Top Performing Salesperson:** Ananya Joshi

## 📊 Dashboard Features

**Sales Analysis**

* Regional Sales Performance
* Product-wise Sales Analysis
* Salesperson Performance
* Monthly Sales Trends

**Profit Analysis**

* Category-wise Profit Contribution
* State-wise Profit Analysis
* Customer Analysis
* Order Status Distribution

## 💡 Business Insights

* The North region contributes the highest sales revenue.
* Laptops and smartphones are major revenue-generating products.
* Electronics is the leading profit-generating category.
* Ananya Joshi leads salesperson-wise sales performance.
* Order status analysis helps identify opportunities to improve operational performance.

## 🎯 Project Outcome

Developed an interactive Power BI dashboard to analyze sales, profitability, product performance, regional trends, and order status, converting raw business data into meaningful visual insights.
